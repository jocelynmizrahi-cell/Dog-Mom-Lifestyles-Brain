# Offer — Dog Mom Lifestyles

## The products
- **Ultimate Box** — monthly subscription box, treats + toy + fun accessory or wellness item for the dog, plus a self-care item for the human. $49.99 a month
- **VIP Box** — the upgraded tier. Higher-value version of the same idea. $69.99 a month
- **T-Shirt Club** — $22.99/mo, ~30 members. Standalone add-on, cute/trendy tee each month.

## The transformation
Not "gets treats and toys." Julie gets a monthly ritual — a fixed point in her month she can count on, where she gets to watch her dog light up over something new, and gets a small surprise for herself bundled in. She never knows exactly what's coming, so opening the box is its own small event: discovering new items and treats neither of them has tried before. The word for it, in her own language: pure joy.

## The path to purchase
Julie sees a Facebook ad, gets emotionally hit or catches the box image, and the reaction is immediate — she buys fast. About 83% of people who convert do so right away, not after a long consideration window. A first-box discount or free gift is typically offered to remove the last bit of friction at that moment.

The discount/free gift is automatic on the page, no code needed. The ad sends her straight to the homepage, which is built to function as the landing page (per Jocelyn: "my home page is made as a landing page").

**Sales page conversion rate: 1.2%** (Jocelyn's real number). This is the missing link that was previously flagged as an unknown gap. At this rate, roughly 1 in 83 visitors becomes a subscriber.

## What happens to people who don't buy
They go onto the email list and SMS list for nurture. (Note: this is a named workflow, not yet detailed here — worth a closer look later at what that nurture sequence actually says and how well it's converting, since that's a lever separate from ad spend.)

## What happens after someone buys
- Extra products, T-shirts, and sweatshirts are available as one-off add-on purchases.
- **Gap, flagged honestly:** There's supposed to be an upgrade path from Ultimate → VIP, but it isn't actually being run right now. This is a real opportunity sitting unused, not a working system — don't treat it as already in place when planning strategy or content.

## Standing creative constraint: the box is a surprise
**Each month's box contents are a surprise and are not revealed in advance.** This is not a limitation to work around, it is the product. Per the transformation above, the not-knowing is what makes opening it an event.

**What this means for every ad, email, and caption:** do not show or list the coming month's contents. Sell the moment the box arrives, the dog's reaction, the ritual of opening it slowly. Past boxes can be shown as proof of variety and quality, but never as a promise of what is coming. Creative that leads with "here is what's inside this month" breaks the product.
